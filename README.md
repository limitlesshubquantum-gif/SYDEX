# SYDEX
### Building the Future of Intelligent Delivery

**SYDEX** is a student-led technology startup focused on developing innovative drone-based delivery solutions for groceries and everyday essentials.

We aim to explore how drone technology can make last-mile delivery more efficient, accessible and reliable.

---

## About Us

SYDEX is a technology-driven startup working at the intersection of drone engineering, software development and logistics innovation.

Our current focus is developing a drone delivery system designed to transport small grocery orders and daily essentials.

We are building our technology through research, experimentation, simulation and prototype development.

## Our Vision

To contribute to the future of intelligent, technology-driven delivery through innovative and accessible drone solutions.

## Our Mission

- Develop innovative drone-based delivery systems.
- Explore affordable and reliable delivery technology.
- Build original software and control-system solutions.
- Research practical applications of drones in everyday logistics.
- Develop and test prototypes through continuous experimentation.

## Our Core Technology

- **Drone Engineering:** Research and development of delivery drone platforms.
- **Flight Systems:** Exploration of embedded systems and flight-control software.
- **IoT and Connectivity:** Research into remote communication and telemetry.
- **Battery Monitoring:** Development of battery-status monitoring systems.
- **Software Development:** Smartphone-based interfaces and monitoring dashboards.

## Research & Development

Our ongoing development areas include:

1. Drone hardware and frame development.
2. ESP32-based embedded systems research.
3. IMU sensor integration and orientation monitoring.
4. Smartphone joystick interface development.
5. Battery monitoring and telemetry.
6. Communication and GPS integration research.

## Development Milestones

- Established the SYDEX drone delivery concept.
- Began developing an experimental drone-control interface.
- Integrated ESP32 and MPU6050 in a simulation environment.
- Explored sensor-based orientation monitoring.
- Started experimenting with battery-voltage monitoring.
- Began planning the first physical prototype.

*These are development milestones, not claims of completed flight testing or commercial deployment.*

## Our Future Roadmap

- Research delivery-system integration.
- Explore practical pilot projects.
